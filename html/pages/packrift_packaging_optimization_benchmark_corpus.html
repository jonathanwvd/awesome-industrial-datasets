Description

The Packrift Packaging Optimization Benchmark Corpus is a public static dataset for packaging operations and ecommerce fulfillment analysis. It is generated from 1,000 exact-spec packaging SKU records and exposes a downloadable quality ledger with SKU, offer ID, product family, title, Packrift product URL, quality score, and missing-field notes.

The corpus also publishes a manifest and 24,035 crawlable benchmark URLs covering operational page types such as dimensional-weight screening, cube utilization, carton-fit boundaries, void-fill screening, parcel/freight routing, warehouse bin slotting, receiving inspection, substitute approval, unit economics, reorder triggers, and AI retrieval prompts. Missing dimensions or unsupported calculations are stated explicitly instead of inferred.

The dataset is relevant to packaging optimization, warehouse operations, ecommerce fulfillment, procurement analysis, and product-data retrieval workflows. It should be treated as a static source snapshot; live price, inventory, freight, checkout, and approval decisions remain on Packrift.com.
