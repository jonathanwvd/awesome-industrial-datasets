# Electrical signals from fixed-speed and variable-speed synchronous generators under healthy and faulty conditions

Voltage and current recordings from three lab synchronous generator benches, fixed and variable speed, with labelled internal faults.

## Description

This dataset gathers voltage and current signals from synchronous generators running in healthy and faulty states on three laboratory benches, at the Engineering School of São Carlos, the Schulich School of Engineering and the Polytechnic School of the University of São Paulo. It comes from the MitDev-Eletrica project of the University of São Paulo's InnovaPower hub, is described in a Data in Brief article and is archived on Zenodo as release v1.0.2 of the project repository.

One bench uses a 2 kVA generator with four salient poles at fixed speed, another a 3 kVA generator with four smooth poles, and the third a salient pole generator at variable speed with a 10 kVA three-level converter on the generator side. Faults are short circuits between turns, between windings of one phase and between phases, applied with limited intensity and duration to protect the machines.

The signals keep the natural noise of an industrial setting, and file names encode the test conditions, which suits fault diagnosis studies. The data is released under CC BY 4.0.
