## Description

This dataset holds time-series measurements from a real water distribution network in Stockholm, Sweden. Its creators are affiliated with KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Uppsala University and Universidade Federal do Ceará. The data underlies the study "Leakage detection in water distribution networks using machine-learning strategies", published by IWA Publishing in 2023.

Operational and hydraulic variables, such as flow and pressure, come from several sensing points in the network and are recorded at regular time intervals. The series cover both normal operating conditions and leakage scenarios. All data is given in the processed form used in the paper, in a single file, SVOA.mat.

The dataset is intended for research on leakage and anomaly detection, time-series analysis and the comparison of centralized and distributed detection approaches. Version v1.0.0 was published on Zenodo in February 2026 under CC BY 4.0, with a request to cite the journal article.
