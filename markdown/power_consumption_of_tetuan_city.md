# Power Consumption of Tetouan City

Ten-minute power consumption of three distribution network zones in Tetouan, Morocco, with temperature, humidity, wind speed and diffuse flows.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Power grids and plants
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Regression
- **Annotation:** Scalar target
- **Access:** UCI repository
- **Size:** 52,417 instances
- **Year:** 2023
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Abdelmalek Essaadi University

## Description

Power Consumption of Tetouan City covers the electricity use of three distribution networks in Tetouan, a city in the north of Morocco. Abdulwahed Salam and Abdelaaziz El Hibaoui of Abdelmalek Essaadi University donated it to the UCI Machine Learning Repository in 2023, and it is introduced in their 2018 paper on comparing machine learning algorithms for power consumption prediction, published at the International Renewable and Sustainable Energy Conference.

The 52,417 instances are taken every ten minutes. Each holds the date and time, temperature, humidity, wind speed, general diffuse flows and diffuse flows as features, with the power consumption of zones 1, 2 and 3 as continuous targets. No values are missing.

The data is used for regression of the zone consumption from the time and weather features. It ships as one CSV file under CC BY 4.0.
