# Peregrine v2021-03

Calibrated layer images from laser fusion, electron beam and binder jet powder bed printers with pixel-wise ground truth labels, 20 layers per technology.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Additive manufacturing
- **Modality:** Image
- **Task:** Defect segmentation; Anomaly localization
- **Annotation:** Pixel mask
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Size:** 20 annotated layers per printing technology
- **Year:** 2023
- **Publisher:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory

## Description

Peregrine v2021-03 holds layer-wise powder bed images from three printing technologies: laser powder bed fusion, electron beam powder bed fusion and binder jetting. Oak Ridge National Laboratory collected the data between 2016 and 2020 and annotated it with Peregrine, a software tool developed internally; it was compiled in March 2021 and carries DOI 10.13139/ORNLNCCS/1779073.

Each technology contributes 20 layers. A layer consists of one or more calibrated images plus an annotation file with pixel-wise ground truth labels drawn by domain experts, typically printer technicians.

The data was assembled mainly for research on anomaly and defect detection by image segmentation or similar techniques. Luke Scime, Vincent Paquit, Chase Joslin, Dylan Richardson, Desarae Goldsby and Larry Lowe are the authors, and the record was published in 2023 with sponsorship from the US Office of Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy, including its Advanced Manufacturing Office, and the Office of Nuclear Energy.
