## Description

Researchers at The University of Manchester and University College London publish this Mendeley Data record as the data for a paper on monitoring and control challenges in an industrial-scale fermentation benchmark. It was produced by IndPenSim, a mathematical model of a fed-batch penicillin fermentation plant that also simulates a Raman spectroscopy device.

The record holds 100 batches with all available process and Raman measurements, about 2.5 GB in total. Batch lengths and control strategies differ: batches 1 to 30 follow a recipe, 31 to 60 are run by operators, 61 to 90 use an advanced process control solution driven by the Raman measurements, and 91 to 100 contain faults that cause process deviations.

A Jupyter notebook on GitHub downloads and plots the data. Goldrick et al. describe the simulator in the Journal of Biotechnology (2015, 193:70-82) and the control challenges in Computers and Chemical Engineering (2019).
