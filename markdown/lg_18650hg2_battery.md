# LG 18650HG2 Li-ion Battery Data

Laboratory test data from a new 3 Ah LG HG2 cell at McMaster University, released with a MATLAB example of a neural network state-of-charge estimator.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Batteries
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Regression
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Publisher:** McMaster University

## Description

LG 18650HG2 Li-ion Battery Data is a set of cell tests carried out by Phillip Kollmeyer at McMaster University in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada, published on Mendeley Data together with an example deep neural network script for estimating the state of charge (SOC) of xEV batteries.

A brand-new 3 Ah LG HG2 cell sat in an 8 cu.ft. thermal chamber and was driven by a Digatron Firing Circuits Universal Battery Tester through a 75 A, 5 V channel with 0.1% of full-scale voltage and current accuracy. The record also describes data acquisition, data preparation and the development of a feedforward neural network (FNN) estimator.

The MATLAB live script trains for 50 epochs with 3 repetitions by default; 5500 epochs and 10 repetitions reproduce the published results. The data, or similar data, appears in a 2020 SAE WCX paper on deep neural network SOC estimation and a 2019 IEEE ITEC paper on LSTM networks with transfer learning.
