# Building Data Genome Project 2

Two years of hourly readings from 3,053 energy meters in 1,636 non-residential buildings at 19 sites in North America and Europe, with building metadata and weather.

- **Domain:** Buildings and appliances
- **Asset / Process:** HVAC and building systems
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Forecasting; Classification
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Code repository
- **Size:** 3,053 energy meters from 1,636 buildings
- **Year:** 2020

## Description

The Building Data Genome Project 2 (BDG2) is an open set of hourly energy meter data from non-residential buildings. It is described in a 2020 Scientific Data article by Clayton Miller and co-authors, and part of it fed the ASHRAE Great Energy Predictor III (GEPIII) competition in late 2019.

It holds 3,053 meters from 1,636 buildings at 19 sites across North America and Europe, covering the full years 2016 and 2017: 17,544 readings per meter, about 53.6 million in all. Each building has one or more meters, which measure electricity, steam, solar energy and heating and cooling water at whole-building level, along with water and irrigation use. The repository keeps raw and cleaned meters, building metadata, weather, a 2017 subset matching the Kaggle competition and example notebooks.

The GEPIII subset of 2,380 meters from 1,449 buildings served long-term prediction for measurement and verification. The data also suit anomaly detection, energy analysis and building type classification.
