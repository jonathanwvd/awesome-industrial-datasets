# XJTU-SY Bearing Datasets

Run-to-failure vibration records of 15 LDK UER204 rolling element bearings from accelerated degradation tests under three speed and radial force conditions.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Bearings
- **Modality:** Vibration
- **Task:** RUL and prognostics

- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Size:** 15 run-to-failure bearings
- **Year:** 2020

## Description

The XJTU-SY Bearing Datasets come from the Institute of Design Science and Basic Component at Xi'an Jiaotong University and Changxing Sumyoung Technology Co., Ltd. They hold complete run-to-failure data of 15 rolling element bearings from accelerated degradation experiments, for validating bearing prognostics. Users are asked to cite a 2020 IEEE Transactions on Reliability paper by Biao Wang, Yaguo Lei, Naipeng Li and Ningbo Li.

The testbed has an AC induction motor, a speed controller, a support shaft, two support bearings and a hydraulic loading system that applies radial force to the tested LDK UER204 bearings. Five bearings ran under each of three conditions: 2100 rpm and 12 kN, 2250 rpm and 11 kN, and 2400 rpm and 10 kN.

Two PCB 352C33 accelerometers at 90° record horizontal and vertical vibration at 25.6 kHz. Each one-minute sampling stores 32,768 points (1.28 s) in a CSV file. Failures include inner race wear, cage fracture, outer race wear and outer race fracture.
