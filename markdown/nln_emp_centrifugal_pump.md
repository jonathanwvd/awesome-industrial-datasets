# Motor Current and Vibration Monitoring Dataset for various Faults in an E-motor-driven Centrifugal Pump

Vibration, motor current and voltage from two electric motor pump set-ups at Fieldlab Techport, with many faults applied at several severity levels.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Pumps, compressors and fans; Motors and drives
- **Modality:** Vibration; Time series
- **Task:** Condition monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Year:** 2024

## Description

This dataset holds experimental measurements from an electric motor driving a centrifugal pump. The data were recorded at Fieldlab Techport within research by the Royal Netherlands Navy, with authors Sietze Bruinsma, Rinze Geertsma, Richard Loendersloot and Tiedo Tinga, together with the University of Twente.

The experiments cover a wide range of faults spread over two set-ups. One set-up ran at three different speeds and the other at a single speed, and most faults were introduced at several levels of severity. The recorded signals are vibration, motor current and voltage.

The data are shared as one archive of 7zipped CSV files, and version 4 was published in August 2024.
