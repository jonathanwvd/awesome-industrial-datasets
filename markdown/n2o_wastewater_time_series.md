# Time Series Dataset for Modeling and Forecasting of N2O in Wastewater Treatment

Two years of nitrous oxide readings every 2 minutes from a full-scale wastewater plant, with influent, effluent, operating and environmental variables for forecasting.

- **Domain:** Water and utilities
- **Asset / Process:** Water treatment and distribution
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Forecasting
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Aalborg Universitet

## Description

This dataset provides two years of nitrous oxide (N2O) measurements from a full-scale wastewater treatment plant, prepared for time series modelling and forecasting. It is published on Mendeley Data by researchers at Aalborg Universitet, and an arXiv paper from 2024 documents it.

Readings are logged every 2 minutes in real time. Besides N2O, the data holds influent and effluent parameters, operating conditions and environmental factors. The paper's statistical analysis points out features that make the series hard to forecast: nonstationarity, nonnormality, seasonality, heteroscedasticity, structural breaks, asymmetric distributions and intermittency.

N2O is a potent greenhouse gas, and the set is meant for models used in real-time operation and control of treatment plants, as well as for testing time series forecasting methods on real process data. Version 4 restores data that was missing during collection and merging, and offers both comma-separated and semicolon-separated files. Mendeley Data lists the record under CC BY 4.0.
