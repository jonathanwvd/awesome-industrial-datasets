# SPARK: High-Resolution Energy Data from a Sustainable Industrial Production Area in Karlsruhe

Electricity measurements every 5 seconds from 22 industrial machines at KIT Campus North, including mills, lathes, chip presses and pumps, over one to seven years.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Energy optimization
- **Access:** Official portal
- **Size:** 22 industrial machines
- **Year:** 2026
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Karlsruhe Institute of Technology

## Description

SPARK is an electricity dataset from the Institute for Data Processing and Electronics (IPE) at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). It holds high-resolution measurements from 22 industrial machines at KIT Campus North, among them mills, lathes, chip presses and pumps. The record was published in 2026 under CC BY 4.0.

Each machine is logged at a 5-second sampling rate, for between one and seven years. The quantities are voltages, currents, active and reactive power, harmonics, total harmonic distortion and frequency. All files are CSV compressed with xz (.csv.xz).

The authors see it as supporting studies of power quality, flexibility and energy use in industry.
