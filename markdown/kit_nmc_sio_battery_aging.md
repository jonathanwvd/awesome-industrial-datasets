## Description

This aging dataset from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) records the capacity and impedance fade of 228 commercial LG INR18650HG2 lithium-ion cells with an NMC cathode and a graphite and silicon oxide anode. Matthias Luh and Thomas Blank cycled the cells on a custom test bench of 19 cycler boards with 12 cells each and describe the data in a 2024 Scientific Data article. It is archived on RADAR4KIT, the KIT data repository.

The cells were aged under 76 operating conditions with three cells each: 16 calendar, 48 cyclic and 12 driving-profile parameter sets at 0, 10, 25 and 40 °C. Check-ups record remaining capacity, impedance spectra from 50 mHz to 14.7 kHz and pulse patterns at five states of charge. Raw voltage, current and temperature logs have a two-second resolution. Version 1 covers 449 days and holds more than 3 billion data rows.

Cells were aged until only 40 to 50% of nominal capacity remained. Version 2 is split into a result data record and a log data record. The data is released under CC BY 4.0.
