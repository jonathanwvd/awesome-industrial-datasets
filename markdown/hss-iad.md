## Description

HSS-IAD (Heterogeneous Same-Sort Industrial Anomaly Detection) was introduced by Qishan Wang, Wenqiang Zhang and five co-authors in a paper at the 2025 IEEE International Conference on Multimedia and Expo (ICME). It targets products of one sort whose parts still vary in structure and appearance, where defects look much like the base material.

Its 8,580 images show metallic-like industrial parts and come with precise anomaly annotations. Besides the defects, the images contain interference such as machining marks and oil stains. HSS-IAD covers three object types (Casting_C1, Casting_C2 and Casting_C3) and four texture types (KolektorSDD, KolektorSDD2, Magnetic-tile-defect and STEEL).

Foreground images are supplied as material for generating synthetic anomalies. The image archive is reached through a download link in the project repository.
