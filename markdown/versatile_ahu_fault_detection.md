# Versatile AHU fault detection - design, field validation and practical application

Building management system records from six air handling units with manually induced faults, split into fault-free and faulty data for FDD validation.

- **Domain:** Buildings and appliances
- **Asset / Process:** HVAC and building systems
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Fault diagnosis
- **Annotation:** Class label; Event and time label
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Size:** 6 air handling units
- **Publisher:** Czech Technical University in Prague

## Description

This dataset from the Czech Technical University in Prague accompanies a paper of the same name in the journal Energy and Buildings. It holds data from 6 air handling units (AHUs) in which faults were induced by hand, so that fault detection and diagnostic tools can be validated against known events.

The records come from the building management system (BMS). Data points were renamed for clarity with three tags taken from the original metadata: a place such as a chiller valve, heat recovery bypass or supply air outlet; a quantity such as air temperature or a 0 to 100 signal; and a type that tells measured values from setpoints. The data is classified into fault-free and faulty parts.

A diagram explains the place tags, and a fault schedule with short descriptions matches the fault classes in the AHU records. The paper gives the details of how the faults were induced and the AHU specifications.
