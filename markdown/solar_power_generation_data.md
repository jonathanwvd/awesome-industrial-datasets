## Description

Solar Power Generation Data contains measurements gathered at two solar power plants in India over a 34-day period. For each plant it pairs the electrical output of the installation with readings from weather sensors on site.

The four files form two pairs, one per plant: Plant_1 and Plant_2 each have a generation file and a weather sensor file. Generation is logged per inverter, and each inverter has several lines of solar panels connected to it. The weather data is logged for the plant as a whole, from a single array of sensors positioned at the site.

The data is aimed at three areas of concern at the plants: predicting power generation for the next couple of days to help manage the grid, recognising when panels need cleaning or maintenance, and finding equipment that is faulty or performs below its potential.
