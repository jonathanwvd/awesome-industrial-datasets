# Hill of Towie wind farm open dataset

Ten-minute SCADA statistics and alarm logs from 21 Siemens turbines at a Scottish wind farm, January 2016 to August 2024, with turbine upgrade dates.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Wind turbines
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Forecasting
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 21 turbines, January 2016 to August 2024
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** RES (Renewable Energy Systems)

## Description

The Hill of Towie wind farm open dataset holds operational data from a commercial wind farm in Scotland with 21 Siemens SWT-2.3-VS-82 turbines. RES released it on Zenodo in 2025 on behalf of TRIG, the owner of the farm, with Alex Clerc and Elizabeth Lingkan of RES as creators.

Yearly archives hold 10-minute statistics and alarm logs from the farm's SCADA system for January 2016 to August 2024, converted to CSV with a few columns dropped for anonymity. Turbine metadata give coordinates, rated power, rotor diameter and hub height, and description files explain the key columns and alarm codes. A separate set gives downtime in seconds for each turbine and 10-minute period, built from SCADA data and RES operator logs.

The record lists the dates of two turbine upgrades: AeroUp, on turbine T13 in September 2021 and on the others from July 2022 to May 2023, and TuneUp on nine test turbines in 2024. Suggested uses include validating energy yield and wake models, performance analysis, alarm prediction and forecasting. The data are released under CC BY 4.0.
