# PreDist Dataset

Operational time series of 93 district heating substations from a German utility, labelled with fault reports, disturbances and maintenance records.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** HVAC and building systems
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Fault diagnosis
- **Annotation:** Event and time label
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 93 substations
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Fraunhofer Institute for Energy Economics and Energy System Technology (IEE)

## Description

PreDist holds operational data of district heating substations run by enercity Netz GmbH, labelled from incident reports and maintenance data. Cyriana Roelofs and colleagues at the Fraunhofer Institute for Energy Economics and Energy System Technology published it on Zenodo in 2025 under CC BY 4.0, with a paper on predictive maintenance in district heating substations.

The time series come from 93 substations of two manufacturers, M1 and M2, and their length depends on when each substation was digitised. Each sub-dataset has a list of faults from incident reports with a short description and problem category, a list of disturbances that also covers corrective and preventive maintenance, feature descriptions and a list of predefined normal events.

Where possible, the reports were extended with a fault label and a note on monitoring potential. The data are meant for developing early fault detection models, and the normal events help evaluate normal behaviour models.
