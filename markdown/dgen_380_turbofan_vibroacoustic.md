# Vibroacoustic dataset of DGEN 380 turbofan test bench

Vibration and microphone recordings from a DGEN 380 turbofan test bench at Safran Tech, with files for two of four measured configurations.

- **Domain:** Aerospace and defense
- **Asset / Process:** Turbines and engines; Gears and gearboxes
- **Modality:** Vibration; Audio
- **Task:** Condition monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Safran Tech

## Description

This dataset from Safran Tech in France holds vibration and acoustic signals from a test bench built around the DGEN 380, a turbofan that is small next to the engines typically fitted to commercial aircraft. In the bench's three-planet planetary gearbox, the carrier stays fixed while power enters through the ring and leaves through the sun.

Three triaxial A/34-2 accelerometers along the engine record vibration in x, y and z, and microphones are placed following ISO 3746 and around components such as the rotor and stator. Four measurement configurations were run, with and without outlet guide vanes (OGVs), across regimes from idle through intermediate speeds to full throttle. The published MATLAB files are named after configuration 2 (normal) and configuration 4 (faulty), with two numbered signal files and two small perfo files for each.

Characteristic frequencies such as blade passing frequencies and the low- and high-pressure shaft frequencies can be tracked in the signals. The data was published in 2024 under CC BY 4.0.
