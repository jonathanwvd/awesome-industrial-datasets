# The Dataset for: Real-time personalized health status prediction of lithium-ion batteries using deep transfer learning

Cycling data of 77 LFP/graphite lithium-ion cells charged the same way but discharged with different multi-stage protocols at a constant 30°C.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Batteries
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Condition monitoring
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Size:** 77 LFP/graphite cells
- **Year:** 2022
- **License:** CC BY

## Description

This battery cycling dataset accompanies the study "Real-time personalized health status prediction of lithium-ion batteries using deep transfer learning". Ye Yuan, Guijun Ma and Songpei Xu, with Xu listed at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, published it on Mendeley Data in May 2022, and the current record is version 2.

It covers 77 LFP/graphite cells, each with a nominal capacity of 1.1 Ah and a nominal voltage of 3.3 V. Every cell followed the same charge protocol, while the discharge side varied between multi-stage protocols, and all cycling ran at a constant 30°C. The data come as a single ZIP archive.

As its title says, the dataset was prepared for that study. The dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.
