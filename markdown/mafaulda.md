## Description

MaFaulDa (Machinery Fault Database) is a collection of multivariate time series acquired on a SpectraQuest machinery fault simulator at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ). Each recording combines accelerometer, tachometer and microphone channels captured at 50 kHz.

The database contains 1,951 time series covering normal operation and several fault conditions of the rotating assembly, including imbalance, misalignment and bearing faults. It is commonly used for vibration-based fault diagnosis, condition monitoring and classification of rotating machinery faults.

The data can be downloaded directly from the project page, either as a complete package of about 13 GB or separately for each fault type.
