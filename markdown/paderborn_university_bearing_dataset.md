## Description

The Paderborn University Bearing Dataset holds experiments with 32 ball bearings of type 6203. Six bearings are undamaged and serve as the healthy reference, twelve carry artificial damage, and fourteen have real damage produced in accelerated lifetime tests.

Motor currents and vibration signals are measured synchronously at a high sampling rate, together with speed, torque, radial load and temperature. Each bearing is run under four operating conditions, with 20 measurements of 4 seconds per setting. Every measurement is a MATLAB file whose name joins the operating condition code and the four-digit bearing code, for example N15_M07_F10_KA01_1.mat.

The bearing damage is described in uniform fact sheets and a measuring log that come with the data, which is offered as a direct download.
