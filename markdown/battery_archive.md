# Battery Archive

Repository that gathers battery cell test data from several institutions for visualization, analysis and comparison, built on the Battery Lifecycle Framework.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Batteries
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Condition monitoring
- **Access:** Request or approval

## Description

Battery Archive is a collection of battery data from several institutions, set up so that the data can be visualized, analysed and compared in one place. It is based on the Battery Lifecycle Framework software, and the work is supported by the Energy Storage Program of the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Electricity, through the Grid Energy Storage Department of Sandia National Laboratories.

The site offers queries and filters for specific experimental conditions, displays of voltage curves and capacity fade, and the option to apply performance and degradation models to the data. A description of the data accompanies the collection. The project states that it publishes more data and updates the Battery Lifecycle Framework software every 2 months.

To obtain the complete CSV files for the cells in a study, the site asks users to email the project, and datasets can be contributed the same way.
