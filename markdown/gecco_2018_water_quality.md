# GECCO Industrial Challenge 2018 Water Quality

Drinking water quality time series from Thüringer Fernwasserversorgung, released for an online anomaly detection competition at GECCO 2018 in Kyoto.

- **Domain:** Water and utilities
- **Asset / Process:** Water treatment and distribution
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Anomaly detection
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Year:** 2018
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** TH Köln

## Description

This dataset was the basis of the GECCO Industrial Challenge 2018, Internet of Things: Online Anomaly Detection for Drinking Water Quality, held at the Genetic and Evolutionary Computation Conference in Kyoto, Japan. The SPOTSeven Lab at TH Köln organised it, and the water utility Thüringer Fernwasserversorgung provided its real-world data through the IMProvT research project.

The task was an anomaly detection algorithm for water and environmental data. The organisers stress that true undesirable variations in water quality must be found while false alarm rates stay very low. The challenge built on the 2017 edition and was the seventh industrial challenge hosted at GECCO.

The Zenodo record holds the water quality CSV, the call for participation, the rules and a resource package, and lists the accepted competition entry abstracts. It is released under CC BY 4.0.
