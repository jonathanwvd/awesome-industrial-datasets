# BattLeDIM

Two years of simulated SCADA flow, pressure and level data with leak records for L-Town, a water network based on a real network in a city in Cyprus, from a leak detection competition.

- **Domain:** Water and utilities
- **Asset / Process:** Water treatment and distribution
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Process monitoring
- **Source Type:** Simulation
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** SCADA data for 2018 and 2019
- **Year:** 2020
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence

## Description

BattLeDIM is the dataset of the Battle of the Leakage Detection and Isolation Methods, a competition that compares ways of detecting and locating leaks in drinking water networks from SCADA flow and pressure sensors. It was organized for the 2nd International CCWI/WDSA Joint Conference in Beijing, which was cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic. Its creators work at the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence, Delft University of Technology, Technion and Tsinghua University.

The problem is set in L-Town, a hypothetical small town with many pipe breaks and water losses. Both its EPANET network model and the SCADA readings derive from an actual distribution network in a Cypriot city. Teams receive a historical year (2018) and an evaluation year (2019), with files of pressures, flows, levels and demands, leak lists and two network models.

The files are on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0. The competition and its results were published in 2022 in the Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management.
