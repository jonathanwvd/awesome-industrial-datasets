# Lithium-Ion-Cell 18650 Labeled Polarity-Aware Anomaly Dataset

Labelled images of cylindrical battery cells from a pack assembly process, marked by polarity and as normal or anomalous for inspection research.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Batteries
- **Modality:** Image
- **Task:** Defect detection
- **Annotation:** Sample label
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Year:** 2026
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Heilbronn University

## Description

This dataset from Heilbronn University contains images of cylindrical battery cells taken during a representative battery pack assembly process under pick-and-place conditions. According to the authors, the scenes vary in placement, background and lighting in ways typical of small and medium-sized manufacturing firms.

A model-assisted pipeline that auto-labels and then verifies the results produced the 256x256-pixel images, each showing one cell cropped from a photo of a populated cell holder. Every image is tagged as positive or negative polarity and sorted into normal or anomalous; anomalies are deviations a person can see, for instance damage or missing insulation.

The main set has a single image of each cell, and a separate extended set adds images under other lighting conditions and positions. The archives also include files named raw images for both sets. The authors present it as a practical resource for supervised inspection and anomaly detection research in assembly vision, valuing feasibility and reuse over standardised benchmarking. Version 0.9 was published in 2026 under CC BY 4.0.
