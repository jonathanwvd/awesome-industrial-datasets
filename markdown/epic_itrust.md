# Electric Power and Intelligent Control (EPIC) Dataset

Sensor, actuator and network captures from a smart grid testbed in Singapore, recorded for 30 minutes under each of eight generation and load scenarios.

- **Domain:** OT security
- **Asset / Process:** Power grids and plants; Control systems and networks
- **Modality:** Time series; Network traffic
- **Task:** Other
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Request or approval
- **Size:** 8 scenarios of 30 minutes
- **Year:** 2020
- **Publisher:** iTrust

## Description

The EPIC dataset comes from the Electric Power and Intelligent Control testbed, a smart grid testbed that iTrust in Singapore added as its third testbed in 2017. The grid has generation, transmission, micro-grid and smart home stages; WAGO PLCs switch breakers and loads. The testbed follows IEC 61850, and its ring network carries GOOSE and MMS messages between relays and SCADA.

The testbed ran for 30 minutes in each of eight scenarios, from synchronising two generators without load, through runs with the PV system switched on and with three generators, to supplying power to iTrust's SWaT and WADI water testbeds. Sensor measurements, actuator states and packet captures were recorded.

The data is described in a CPS4CIP 2020 paper on a smart grid dataset for machine-learning security research. iTrust gives it free of charge to researchers worldwide through its dataset request form.
