# ASHRAE - Great Energy Predictor III

Kaggle competition run by ASHRAE in late 2019 on predicting building energy use from hourly meter readings of 2,380 meters in 1,449 buildings.

- **Domain:** Buildings and appliances
- **Asset / Process:** HVAC and building systems
- **Modality:** Time series; Tabular
- **Task:** Forecasting
- **Access:** Competition
- **Size:** 2,380 meters from 1,449 buildings
- **Year:** 2019
- **Publisher:** ASHRAE
- **Benchmark:** Yes

## Description

ASHRAE - Great Energy Predictor III (GEPIII) is a featured Kaggle competition organised by ASHRAE around one question: how much energy will a building consume? It ran from October to December 2019, offered a reward of 25,000 USD and drew 3,614 teams.

Its data are a subset of the Building Data Genome Project 2: hourly readings from 2,380 meters in 1,449 buildings, which the competition used to predict energy use over the long term, as an application to measurement and verification in building energy analysis. The competition files hold training and test meter tables, building metadata, weather data for the training and test periods, and a sample submission.

Submissions are scored by root mean squared logarithmic error. The full Building Data Genome Project 2, with 3,053 meters from 1,636 buildings, is published separately.
