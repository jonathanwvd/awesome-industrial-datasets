# Internal Defect Database of Deformed Ferritic Steel (X-CT)

X-ray computed tomography volumes of tensile and fatigue tested ferritic steel samples, with void and crack descriptors quantified by persistent homology.

- **Domain:** Materials and metrology
- **Modality:** X-ray and CT; Tabular
- **Task:** Other
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 134 tensile and 142 fatigue samples (195.47 GB)
- **Year:** 2025

## Description

This database documents internal defects that form in ferritic steel during mechanical deformation. Gunjick Lee, Gyeong Hoon Yi, Leslie Ching Ow Tiong and co-authors scanned deformed samples with X-ray computed tomography on a ZEISS Xradia 520 Versa at a 3 µm step size, and published the data on Dryad with a descriptor in Scientific Data (2025).

There are two parts. The tensile part holds 938 defect features from 134 samples, with local strain measured by digital image correlation and grouped in 20% bands from 0% to above 120%. The fatigue part holds 2,305 defect features from 142 samples, scanned repeatedly during interrupted fatigue tests until fracture and grouped by fracture progress.

Each sample folder has raw and processed X-CT images, 3D visualisation data and histograms of defect size and spacing. A summary spreadsheet lists, per sample, void density by size range, inter-defect distance and, for fatigue, stress, cycle counts and crack thickness and depth grades. Void density, size and spacing were derived through persistent homology.
