## Description

This dataset holds dimensional measurements of parts produced by the 20 operators of one industry during a single period of time. It is released under CC0 1.0 and consists of one CSV file, Piece_Dimension.csv.

Each operator produced 500 parts. Every row describes one part in five columns: an item number (Item_No), the measured Length, Width and Height of the part, and the Operator who made it.

The operators have different training backgrounds, and the Operator column ties every measurement to the worker who produced the part. The perfect, nominal measures of the part are left out, so the data holds only the measured dimensions of each piece.
