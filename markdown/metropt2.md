# MetroPT2

Three months of 1 Hz analogue, digital and GPS signals from the air compressor of a Porto metro train in 2022, unlabelled but with two failure reports.

- **Domain:** Transportation and mobility
- **Asset / Process:** Pumps, compressors and fans; Vehicles and fleets
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Anomaly detection; RUL and prognostics
- **Annotation:** Unlabeled
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 7,116,940 instances, 21 attributes
- **Year:** 2022
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** INESC TEC

## Description

MetroPT2 comes from an explainable predictive maintenance project with the urban metro service of Porto, Portugal. Bruno Veloso, João Gama, Rita Ribeiro and Pedro Pereira of INESC TEC published it on Zenodo in 2022 under CC BY 4.0.

An onboard embedded device logged the compressor of a train at 1 Hz between 28 April and 28 July 2022. The 16 signals include pressures, motor current, oil temperature, a flowmeter and the electrical signals of the air intake valves; the record also names digital control signals and GPS latitude, longitude and speed. It gives 7,116,940 instances and 21 attributes.

The data carry no labels, but the operator's failure reports give the start and end of an air leak in June 2022 and an oil leak in July 2022. The data target online anomaly detection and failure prediction, as well as remaining useful life estimation.
