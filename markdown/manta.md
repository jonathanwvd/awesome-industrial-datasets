## Description

MANTA is an anomaly detection dataset for tiny objects that pairs a visual part with a text part. It was introduced in an arXiv paper submitted in December 2024 by Lei Fan, Dongdong Fan, Zhiguang Hu, Yiwen Ding, Donglin Di, Kai Yi, Maurice Pagnucco and Yang Song.

The visual part holds more than 137.3K images of 38 object categories from five typical domains. Each object is photographed from five distinct viewpoints, and 8.6K images are labelled as anomalous with pixel-level annotations. A tiny version keeps 800 images per class.

The text part has two subsets. Declarative Knowledge uses 875 words to describe common anomalies by domain and category, explaining what they are, why they occur and how they look. Constructivist Learning offers 2K multiple-choice questions of varying difficulty, each paired with images and an answer explanation. The paper adds a baseline for the visual-text tasks. The data is downloaded from figshare under a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
