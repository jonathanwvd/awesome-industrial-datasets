# SCANIA Component X Dataset

Multivariate time series, repair records and specifications for an anonymized engine component across a fleet of Scania trucks in Sweden.

- **Domain:** Transportation and mobility
- **Asset / Process:** Vehicles and fleets
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** RUL and prognostics; Classification
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Scania CV AB

## Description

This dataset follows one anonymized engine component, named Component X, across a fleet of trucks from Scania in Sweden. Scania CV AB publishes it as multivariate time series of operational data.

Next to the operational readouts, the files hold repair records and the specifications of each truck, all anonymized to protect confidentiality. Many features take the form of histograms and numerical counters, and the readouts keep their temporal information over a large population of vehicles. Version 2, published in July 2024, adds the labels of the test set.

The publisher lists classification, regression, survival analysis and anomaly detection as applications, with predictive maintenance as the main setting, and aims to foster reproducible research. The files are openly accessible under CC BY 4.0.
