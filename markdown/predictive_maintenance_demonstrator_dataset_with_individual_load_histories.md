## Description

This dataset comes from a predictive maintenance demonstrator at Technische Universität Dresden in which component failure occurs according to individual load histories. Notched steel specimens are clamped between two servo-hydraulic cylinders of a fatigue test bench, and the test runs are recorded with acceleration, force, and displacement sensors.

The demonstrator is designed as a virtual-sensing use case, where unmeasured physical quantities are predicted from the available measurements, and it provides independent training and testing data for developing and benchmarking predictive maintenance and failure prediction methods. The dataset was created within the LRVTwin project on digital twins for light rail vehicles.

The data were published in 2025 by Heindel, Hantschke and Kästner on the OPARA research data repository of TU Dresden under CC BY 4.0, with full documentation included in the archive.
