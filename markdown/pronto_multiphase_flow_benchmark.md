# PRONTO heterogeneous benchmark dataset

Process, alarm, high-frequency ultrasonic and video data from an industrial-scale multiphase flow facility run with and without induced faults.

- **Domain:** Chemical and process
- **Modality:** Time series; Video; Multimodal
- **Task:** Process monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Year:** 2019
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** ABB Corporate Research Center, Poland

## Description

The PRONTO heterogeneous benchmark dataset comes from an industrial-scale multiphase flow facility. Anna Stief and James R. Ottewill of the ABB Corporate Research Center in Poland, Ruomu Tan of Imperial College London and Yi Cao of Zhejiang University published it on Zenodo in 2019 under CC BY 4.0.

The facility was run under various operating conditions, with and without induced faults, to produce multi-rate and multi-modal data. Besides process measurements and alarm records, it holds ultrasonic flow and pressure measurements taken at high frequency, video recordings and an operation log. A technical report on the case study and a README come with the data archive.

The publishers present the data for developing and validating fault detection and diagnosis and data fusion methods. The reference paper appeared in the Journal of Process Control, volume 79 (2019), pages 41 to 55; the record also lists IFAC papers from 2018 and 2019 that use the data, some of them then in press.
