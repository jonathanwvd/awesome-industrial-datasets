## Description

This dataset tracks the daily operation of a single oil well, well 807, in an oil field in the north of Russia over 2013 to 2021. The well was drilled in 2013 to a depth of 2400 meters, and the data was collected by a lead geologist working on a Siberian oil field.

Columns cover oil, liquid, gas and water volumes in cubic metres per day, the water cut in percent from a daily surface sample, the dynamic level in metres and the reservoir pressure in atmospheres from a sensor in the pump. A metering unit gives the liquid and gas volumes, and the oil and water volumes are calculated from them. Everything sits in one Excel workbook.

As reservoir pressure falls over the life of the well, oil and gas output drops and the water share rises. The geologist's stated aim is to predict this decline in oil production and to decide when to act to raise it.
