# Order Picking Dataset from a Warehouse of a Footwear Manufacturing Company

Anonymized order picking records, layouts and storage data from a footwear manufacturer's warehouse, for picker routing, order batching, wave generation and AGV studies.

- **Domain:** Logistics and retail
- **Modality:** Tabular; Geospatial and remote sensing
- **Task:** Scheduling and optimization
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** École de technologie supérieure

## Description

This dataset comes from the warehouse of a real footwear manufacturer. It was extracted with SQL queries from the company's warehouse management system and published on Mendeley Data in 2024 by a researcher at École de technologie supérieure in Montreal.

The warehouse is mapped on Cartesian coordinates, with storage locations, aisles and central depots. Files cover product classes, storage positions, picking waves and routing paths, under random, class-based, dedicated and hybrid storage policies. Timestamps and per-operator performance show how the operation runs over time. The data was anonymized and randomized while keeping realistic patterns, and it comes as CSV tables, CAD layouts and Python code.

The authors suggest it for optimization work on picker routing, order batching and wave generation, and its navigation data also suits studies of automated guided vehicles and other robots in warehouses. It is released under CC BY 4.0.
