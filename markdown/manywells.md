## Description

ManyWells is a project by Solution Seeker AS that shares a simulator of multiphase flow in vertical oil and gas wells together with datasets generated by it. Its paper "ManyWells: Simulation of multiphase flow in thousands of wells" by Bjarne Grimstad, Erlend Lundby and Henrik Andersson appeared in Geoenergy Science and Engineering in 2026.

The code implements a steady-state drift-flux model of liquid and gas flow, treating oil and water as one mixed liquid phase. Each well runs from bottomhole pressure to downstream choke pressure, with an inflow model and a choke model as boundary conditions. The project also holds code for calibration to data, closed-loop control simulation and a flow regime classifier.

Three datasets on Hugging Face cover different operating conditions: stationary boundary conditions with open-loop control (manywells-sol-1), non-stationary conditions with open-loop control (manywells-nsol-1) and non-stationary conditions with closed-loop control (manywells-nscl-1). Each holds one million data points from 2,000 vertical wells, with pressures, temperatures, flow rates, choke position and flow regime (bubbly, slug-churn or annular). Code and data are licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0.
