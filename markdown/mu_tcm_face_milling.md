# MU-TCM face-milling dataset

Synchronized vibration, force, acoustic emission and CNC signals with tool wear levels from 32 face-milling experiments on cast iron and stainless steel.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** CNC machining and tool wear
- **Modality:** Vibration; Time series
- **Task:** Condition monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Size:** 32 experiments, each repeated twice
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Mondragon Unibertsitatea

## Description

The MU-TCM face-milling dataset holds signals and tool wear levels recorded while milling parts of two materials. Mondragon Unibertsitatea published it in 2025, with data collected in Arrasate-Mondragon, Gipuzkoa, Spain, by José Joaquín Peralta Abadía, Mikel Cuesta Zabalajauregui and Felix Larrinaga.

Cutting took place on a Lagun GVC 1000-HS with AYMA inserts, on cast iron machined dry and on stainless steel with minimum quantity lubrication. The plan gives 32 experiments, each repeated twice, at 1.5 mm axial and 58.4 mm radial depth of cut. Vibration and force were sampled at 50 kHz and acoustic emission at 1 MHz, while internal CNC signals came at 250 Hz. Tool wear (VB) was measured with a macroscope at the start of each experiment.

The synchronized signals are stored as .mat files and the CSV files use semicolon separators; features extracted from the signals support model training for tool condition monitoring. A small subset with eight experiments lets users try the data before downloading the full archive. The license is CC BY 4.0.
