# Ismailia Sewage Treatment Plant operational monitoring dataset, 2000–2008

Daily influent, operating and effluent records from a full-scale aerated-lagoon sewage plant in Ismailia, Egypt, across three lagoon stages from 2000 to 2008.

- **Domain:** Water and utilities
- **Asset / Process:** Water treatment and distribution
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Process monitoring
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Size:** 8,211 complete observations
- **Year:** 2026
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Majmaah University

## Description

This dataset holds daily operating records of the Ismailia Sewage Treatment Plant (ISTP) in Serabioum, Ismailia Governorate, Egypt, which treats wastewater at full scale in a chain of aerated lagoons. It was uploaded by the corresponding author of a study that uses interpretable machine learning and rolling-origin validation to separate control and predictability regimes in the lagoons' performance. Its listed institution is Majmaah University.

The data spans 2000 to 2008, with 8,211 complete observations from 2,737 sampling dates. They follow three lagoon stages in series: aerated, facultative and maturation. Variables cover the influent, plant operation and the effluent of each stage, among them flow, temperature, pH, DO, BOD5, TSS, VSS and fecal coliform.

The files come in CSV and Excel formats, and the Excel workbook includes a data dictionary that explains each variable. The record was published on Mendeley Data in 2026 under the CC BY 4.0 license.
