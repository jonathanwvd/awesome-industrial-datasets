# Big data collection in pharmaceutical manufacturing

Raw material, tablet compression time series and final quality data for 1005 production batches of a cholesterol-lowering medicine, from November 2018 to April 2021.

- **Domain:** Chemical and process
- **Modality:** Time series; Tabular
- **Task:** Quality prediction
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 1005 production batches
- **Year:** 2021

## Description

This dataset describes the manufacture of one pharmaceutical product across all relevant process steps. It comes from Janja Žagar of Lek Pharmaceuticals d.d. in Ljubljana, Slovenia, and Jurij Mihelič of the University of Ljubljana, and accompanies a data article on its use for product quality predictions.

The product is a high-volume medicine for lowering blood cholesterol, made as film-coated tablets with an immediate release profile. The data cover 1005 production batches from November 2018 to April 2021: properties of incoming raw materials, compression time series such as tablet press speed, filling device speed, main and pre-compression force and tablet fill depth, and final quality results including drug release, impurities and related substances.

Laboratory results sit in one CSV file and the time series in one file per product code. The authors recommend the data for predicting final product quality.
