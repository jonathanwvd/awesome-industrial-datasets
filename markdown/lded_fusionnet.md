## Description

The LDED-FusionNet Dataset holds multisensor audio-visual data from laser-directed energy deposition (LDED), a metal additive manufacturing process. It was published on Zenodo in 2025 by Lequn Chen of the Agency for Science, Technology and Research and accompanies the LDED-FusionNet code repository on GitHub.

The recordings pair coaxial melt pool images with multi-domain acoustic signals captured during LDED processing. Robot tool-center-point (TCP) motion logs add the spatial position of the process. Ground truth annotations cover melt pool stability, defect types and process variations.

The data is intended for real-time defect detection, acoustic-visual correlations for quality prediction and multisensor fusion research. It has been used in papers on a multisensor fusion-based digital twin (RCIM 2023), in-situ defect detection with multi-sensor fusion (JMST 2024) and melt pool inference from acoustic features (ICCAR 2024). It is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
