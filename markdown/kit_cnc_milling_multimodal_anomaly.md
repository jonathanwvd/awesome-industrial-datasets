# A Multimodal Dataset for Process Monitoring and Anomaly Detection in Industrial CNC Milling

Controller signals plus external force and spindle acceleration from 33 milling experiments on a DMC 60 H machine, covering eight anomaly types.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** CNC machining and tool wear
- **Modality:** Time series; Vibration; Multimodal
- **Task:** Process monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Official portal
- **Size:** 33 milling experiments
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Karlsruhe Institute of Technology

## Description

This dataset from the Institute of Production Science (WBK) at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) records about six hours of milling on a DMC 60 H three-axis horizontal milling machine from Deckel Maho. It was published in 2025 under CC BY 4.0.

The 33 milling experiments span three component types and eight anomaly types, in S235JR structural steel, Al2007 T4 aluminium alloy and POM-C. Process signals from the Siemens SINUMERIK 840D controller are exported at 500 Hz. A Kistler Type 9255C force platform and a PCB Type 356A33 accelerometer on the main spindle add force and acceleration at 10 kHz. A machine log file gives the executed NC code, and a header file holds recording metadata.

NC programs, 3D CAD models (.stp) and documentation come with the data so the experiments can be reproduced.
