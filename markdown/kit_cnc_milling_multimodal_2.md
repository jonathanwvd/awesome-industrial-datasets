# A Multimodal Dataset 2 for Process Monitoring and Anomaly Detection in Industrial CNC Milling

Process data from 54 experiments on a three-axis DMG Mori milling machine: 500 Hz controller signals, 10 kHz force and acceleration, NC programs and pictures.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** CNC machining and tool wear
- **Modality:** Time series; Vibration; Image
- **Task:** Process monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Size:** 54 milling experiments
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Karlsruhe Institute of Technology

## Description

This dataset holds process data from milling on a three-axis DMG Mori CMX 600V machine and complements an earlier multimodal milling dataset from the Institute of Production Science (wbk) at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology. It was published in October 2025 by Robin Ströbel, Maximilian Kuck and Florian Oexle.

It contains 54 milling experiments with components, tools and machining strategies chosen to be relevant to industry, recorded in a controlled laboratory setting. Signals from the Siemens SINUMERIK 840D controller were captured with the SINUMERIK Edge app Analyse MyWorkpiece/Capture at 500 Hz and stored as JSON. A force platform and a spindle-mounted accelerometer added force and acceleration signals at 10 kHz. NC programs, labelled pictures and detailed documentation are included to support reproducibility and benchmarking.

The record is tagged for process monitoring and anomaly detection on machine tools, and the data is released under CC BY 4.0.
