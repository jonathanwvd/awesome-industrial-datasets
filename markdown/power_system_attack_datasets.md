# Power System Datasets

Three datasets of electric transmission system normal, disturbance, control and cyber attack behaviour, with synchrophasor measurements plus logs from Snort, a simulated control panel and relays.

- **Domain:** OT security
- **Asset / Process:** Power grids and plants
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Intrusion detection; Classification
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Size:** 3 datasets

## Description

The Power System Datasets are three datasets created by Uttam Adhikari, Shengyi Pan and Tommy Morris together with Raymond Borges and Justin Beaver of Oak Ridge National Laboratories (ORNL). They form Dataset 1 of an industrial control system cyber attack collection that also holds gas pipeline, water storage tank and energy management system data.

The measurements describe normal, disturbance, control and cyber attack behaviour of an electric transmission system. They combine synchrophasor measurements with data logs from Snort, a simulated control panel and relays.

The datasets have supported several studies on power system cyber-attack classification, among them a hybrid intrusion detection system for power systems in IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid, the classification of disturbances and cyber-attacks from heterogeneous time-synchronized data in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, and a paper on machine learning for disturbance and cyber-attack discrimination at the 7th International Symposium on Resilient Control Systems.
