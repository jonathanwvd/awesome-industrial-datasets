# Industrial Control System (ICS) Cyber Attack Datasets

Five cyber attack datasets from Tommy Morris and ORNL partners covering a power transmission system, laboratory gas pipeline and water storage tank, and an energy management system log.

- **Domain:** OT security
- **Asset / Process:** Power grids and plants; Wells, pipelines and reservoirs; Control systems and networks
- **Modality:** Time series; Network traffic
- **Task:** Intrusion detection; Classification
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Size:** 5 datasets

## Description

Tommy Morris and colleagues publish five industrial control system cyber attack datasets on one page. The Power System Datasets, made with Uttam Adhikari, Shengyi Pan and Raymond Borges and Justin Beaver of Oak Ridge National Laboratories (ORNL), are three sets of electric transmission system normal, disturbance, control and attack behaviour, combining synchrophasor measurements with logs from Snort, a simulated control panel and relays.

The Gas Pipeline Datasets, made with Justin Beaver and Raymond Borges of ORNL, are gas pipeline SCADA logs formatted at ORNL. Wei Gao and Tommy Morris built attack datasets on two laboratory-scale control systems, a gas pipeline and a water storage tank; the page warns that these contain unintended patterns, such as attacks run at another pressure than normal operation, that make attacks unrealistically easy to spot. Ian Turnipseed added gas pipeline sets with more randomness.

The fifth set is a 30-day anonymised energy management system log shared by an investor-owned utility. The sets have supported work on power system intrusion detection and on one-class classification for SCADA systems.
