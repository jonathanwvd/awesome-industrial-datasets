# Wastewater Treatment Plant Data for Nutrient Removal System

SCADA time series sampled every two minutes at the Agtrup (BlueKolding) wastewater plant in Denmark, covering control, process and environmental variables of phosphorus removal.

- **Domain:** Water and utilities
- **Asset / Process:** Water treatment and distribution
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Forecasting
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Year:** 2024
- **License:** CC BY-NC

## Description

This dataset comes from the Agtrup (BlueKolding) wastewater treatment plant in Denmark, a plant designed to strengthen phosphorus removal through chemical and biological methods. Aalborg Universitet and Veolia Water Solutions and Technologies are the listed institutions, and a data paper on arXiv from 2024 describes it.

The data was collected at high frequency from the plant's Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system. It forms time series of control, process and environmental variables linked to the operation of nutrient removal, with every measurement sampled at a two-minute interval.

The authors built it for predictive models and for optimizing operating strategies in wastewater treatment. Their stated aim is to support digital twins and machine learning methods such as deep reinforcement learning that predict and improve plant performance under changing conditions, with attention to how chemical and biological phosphorus removal interact. Mendeley Data lists the record under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 Unported license, so commercial use is not covered.
