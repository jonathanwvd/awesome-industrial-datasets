# IMS Bearings

Bearing experiments from the University of Cincinnati's Center for Intelligent Maintenance Systems, distributed in NASA's Prognostics Data Repository.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Bearings
- **Modality:** Vibration
- **Task:** RUL and prognostics; Condition monitoring
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Year:** 2007
- **Publisher:** NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE)

## Description

IMS Bearings is a set of bearing experiments contributed by the Center for Intelligent Maintenance Systems (IMS) at the University of Cincinnati. NASA's Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE) at Ames Research Center publishes it on the NASA Open Data Portal as one of the entries of its Prognostics Data Repository, a collection of donated data sets intended for building prognostic algorithms.

The data is cited as the Bearing Data Set by J. Lee, H. Qiu, G. Yu, J. Lin and Rexnord Technical Services (2007), IMS, University of Cincinnati. It is distributed as a single ZIP archive from the repository. Work that uses it is asked to acknowledge both the repository and the donors, and neither NASA nor the donors accept liability for its use.
