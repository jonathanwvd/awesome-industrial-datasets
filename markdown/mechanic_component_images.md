## Description

Mechanic Component Images ( Normal / Defected) is an image collection for quality recognition of pistons, a mechanical component used in household air conditioners. Pistons are produced by many manufacturers worldwide and are usually checked by quality operators; the stated goal is to automate that check with AI. The uploader, satishpaladi11, collected the images manually from different components.

The PNG images, with file names such as kumda_component1.png, are organised in three groups. Defected 1 holds pistons that are broken, out of shape or fallen. Defected 2 holds pistons with oil, grease or rust stains. Normal holds pistons without defects.

The group names act as labels, giving two defect types and one normal class for the same part.
