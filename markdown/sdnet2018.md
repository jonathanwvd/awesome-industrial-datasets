# SDNET2018

Over 56,000 annotated image patches of cracked and uncracked concrete bridge decks, walls and pavements, labelled per patch for crack detection research.

- **Domain:** Infrastructure and civil
- **Modality:** Image
- **Task:** Defect detection
- **Annotation:** Sample label
- **Size:** Over 56,000 images
- **Year:** 2018
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Utah State University

## Description

SDNET2018 (Structural Defects Network 2018) is an annotated image set for training, validating and benchmarking crack detection algorithms for concrete. Utah State University published it in May 2018 under a CC BY 4.0 license.

The images derive from 230 photographs of cracked and uncracked concrete taken with a 16 MP Nikon camera: 54 of bridge decks at the university's SMASH laboratory, 72 of walls of the Russell/Wanlass Performance Hall and 104 of campus roads and sidewalks. Each photograph is cut into 256 × 256 px subimages, labelled C when a crack is present and U when it is not, giving over 56,000 images. Cracks range from 0.06 mm to 25 mm wide.

Some images carry obstructions such as shadows, surface roughness, scaling, edges, holes and background debris. The files come as JPEG images and text files.
