# 18650 Cell Characterization Dataset: Electrical Parameters and Visual Inspection from CERBEA Project

Electrical parameters and photographs of 360 recycled 18650 lithium-ion cells from various manufacturers, gathered for battery recycling and reuse research.

## Description

This dataset gathers basic electrical parameters and photographs of 360 recycled lithium-ion cells in the 18650 format. It comes from CERBEA, a research project on the intelligent recycling of lithium batteries to build home energy accumulators within a circular economy. The creators are affiliated with the Universidad de Salamanca and the Universidad Pontificia de Salamanca.

The cells come from several manufacturers and models. They were measured between 2023 and 2024 with standardized test procedures and calibrated equipment. The table is a CSV file with 360 rows and 8 columns, one row per cell, and every record has a photograph that documents the physical condition of the cell. The images ship in a separate ZIP archive.

The data serves research on the recycling and second use of 18650 cells. It was published on Zenodo in February 2025 under CC BY 4.0.
