# Multimodal dataset for wind turbine blade monitoring during lightning strikes

Fiber optic vibration, load and strain measurements of wind turbine blades during lightning strikes, with operating status records and post-strike damage assessments.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Wind turbines
- **Modality:** Time series; Vibration
- **Task:** Condition monitoring
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** Six CSV files of load, strain, vibration and temperature data
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY

## Description

MDWTBM-LS is a multimodal dataset that follows wind turbine blades through lightning strikes, which threaten both the structure of a blade and its operating performance. Six authors deposited it on figshare in 2025, with funding from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Natural Science Foundation of Hunan Province.

Vibration, load and strain readings come from fiber optic load sensors and fiber optic accelerometers. Alongside them, the data hold real-time records of each blade's operating status and damage assessments made after the strikes. The record groups load, strain, vibration and temperature tables, extended load and strain tables, and a README in one MDWTBM-LS folder.

The authors aim the data at predictive models that detect lightning strikes and assess blade damage, and name condition monitoring and fault prediction among the research fields it serves. It is released under CC BY 4.0.
