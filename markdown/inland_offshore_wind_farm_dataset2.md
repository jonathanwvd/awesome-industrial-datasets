# Inland-Offshore Wind Farm Dataset2

Ten-minute data covering the first four years of operation of turbines at an inland and an offshore wind farm, with met mast environmental variables.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Wind turbines
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Other
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 4 years per farm, 10-minute data
- **Year:** 2021
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Texas A&M University

## Description

Inland-Offshore Wind Farm Dataset2 is the second inland and offshore wind farm dataset that Yu Ding of Texas A&M University published on Zenodo in 2021. It covers the first four years of operation of the turbines.

The 10-minute data runs from 2008 to 2011 for the inland turbines and from 2007 to 2010 for the offshore ones. The inland data uses the same environmental variables as the Inland Wind Farm Dataset1 and the offshore data those of the Offshore Wind Farm Dataset1. Most environmental measurements come from the nearest met mast, but wind speed and turbulence intensity are measured on the turbine; mast air pressure and temperature feed the air density.

The data is released under CC BY 4.0, and each turbine's records cover several consecutive years.
