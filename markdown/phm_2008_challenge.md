# PHM 2008 Challenge

Multivariate time series of aircraft engine degradation from the PHM08 prognostics competition, split into training and test subsets.

- **Domain:** Aerospace and defense
- **Asset / Process:** Turbines and engines
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** RUL and prognostics
- **Year:** 2008

## Description

The PHM 2008 Challenge data describes the degradation of aircraft engines. It was used in the prognostics challenge competition of the International Conference on Prognostics and Health Management (PHM08), and the challenge stays open so that researchers can compare their results with those of the 2008 winners.

The data consists of several data sets of multivariate time series, each divided into training and test subsets. Every time series comes from a different engine of the same type, so together they form a fleet. Each engine starts with its own degree of initial wear and manufacturing variation, which is not given to the user and counts as normal rather than as a fault.

Three operational settings with a substantial effect on engine performance are included in the data, and the measurements contain sensor noise.
