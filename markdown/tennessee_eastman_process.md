# Tennessee Eastman Challenge Archive

Download archive of the Tennessee Eastman challenge process simulator in Fortran, C, MATLAB and Simulink, with recorded responses to 15 process disturbances.

- **Domain:** Chemical and process
- **Asset / Process:** Chemical process units
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Simulation and control
- **Source Type:** Simulation
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Publisher:** University of Washington

## Description

The Tennessee Eastman Challenge Archive collects code and data for the Tennessee Eastman (TE) process simulation. It is maintained by N. Lawrence Ricker, Professor Emeritus of Chemical Engineering at the University of Washington, and holds the basic TE code with the Fortran model provided by Tennessee Eastman, MATLAB and Simulink interfaces, a MATLAB conversion by the Rivera group, nonlinear MPC code and a simplified TE process.

An updated C version of January 23, 2015 allows variable-step integration in MATLAB/Simulink, documents most internal variables and adds optional disturbances and measurements; it was tested with MATLAB/Simulink 2014b. A tables archive gives operating conditions at 6 steady states from Ricker's 1995 paper on optimal steady-state operation.

Fifteen archives, idv1 to idv15, record how the decentralized control strategy of Ricker (1996) responds to each disturbance of the original problem by Downs and Vogel, from a step in the A/C feed ratio to sticking cooling water valves. Many of the codes were written for MATLAB 3.x and 4.x.
