# PHM Europe 2026 Conference Data Challenge

Hall sensor recordings from a servomotor test bench of a subway ticket validation door, released to estimate the remaining useful life of door position degradation.

- **Domain:** Transportation and mobility
- **Asset / Process:** Motors and drives
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** RUL and prognostics
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Competition
- **Size:** 1.6GB .zip file
- **Year:** 2026

## Description

The PHM Europe 2026 Conference Data Challenge, hosted on the PHM Society data repository, is built around a test bench with a rotating machine: the servomotor that drives a subway ticket validation door. The goal set for teams is to estimate the remaining useful life (RUL) linked to the degradation of the door position.

On the bench, a three-phase brushless motor from Crouzet Motors Industry in France moves the door, and a controller developed by INGENIA industry in Spain carries out the door movements. Data are taken from the hall sensors installed in the system.

The whole dataset is one 1.6GB zip archive, accompanied by a text description of the challenge and data format and by a slide presentation. Submissions are ranked on a public leaderboard whose score reaches 1 for a perfect result.
