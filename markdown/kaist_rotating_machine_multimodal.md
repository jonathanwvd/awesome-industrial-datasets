# Vibration, Acoustic, Temperature, and Motor Current Dataset of Rotating Machine

Synchronised vibration, acoustic, temperature and three-phase motor current signals of a rotating machine with bearing faults, misalignment and unbalance at three loads.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Bearings; Motors and drives
- **Modality:** Vibration; Audio; Time series
- **Task:** Fault diagnosis
- **Annotation:** Class label
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Year:** 2023
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology

## Description

This Mendeley Data record holds time-series measurements of a rotating machine under varying loads, gathered for fault diagnosis by Wonho Jung, Seong-Hu Kim, SungHyun Yun, Jaewoong Bae and Yong-Hwa Park of the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST). It was published in 2023 and is described further in an article in Data in Brief.

Besides the normal state, the machine runs with a faulty inner or outer bearing race, a misaligned shaft or an unbalanced rotor, each under three load conditions. File names encode the load in Nm, the condition and its severity. Vibration files give x and y directions at housings A and B in g; acoustic files give sound pressure in pascals. A third set, in TDMS format, holds temperature of both housings and the U, V and W phase motor currents.

The data is split into three archives, one each for vibration, acoustic, and current with temperature, and is released under CC BY 4.0.
