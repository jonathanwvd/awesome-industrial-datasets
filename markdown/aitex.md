## Description

The AITEX Fabric Image Database is a public, annotated collection of fabric images for machine vision inspection of textiles. It was presented in the 2019 article "A Public Fabric Database for Defect Detection Methods and Results" in AUTEX Research Journal, written by Javier Silvestre-Blanes, Teresa Albero-Albero, Ignacio Miralles, Ruben Pérez-Llorens and Jorge Moreno, whose affiliations include the Escuela Politécnica Superior de Alcoy.

The collection gathers images both with and without defects, so that new detection and classification methods can be compared on one shared public set instead of on small private image collections. The article also reviews several defect detection methods and applies one of them to the images, reporting its results.

The work was funded by IVACE and the European Regional Development Fund through the AUTOVIMOTION and INTELITEX projects. The article appears in volume 19, issue 4 of the journal, under DOI 10.2478/aut-2019-0035.
