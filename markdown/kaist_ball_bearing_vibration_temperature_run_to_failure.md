# Ball Bearing Vibration, and Temperature Run-to-Failure Dataset

Hourly vibration and temperature snapshots from a 128-hour accelerated life test of an NSK 6205 ball bearing run until failure at KAIST.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Bearings
- **Modality:** Vibration; Time series
- **Task:** RUL and prognostics; Condition monitoring
- **Annotation:** RUL label
- **Source Type:** Lab or testbed
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Size:** 129 hourly CSV files
- **Publisher:** Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)

## Description

This dataset from the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) follows a ball bearing from normal operation to failure in an accelerated life test. It accompanies a Data in Brief article on run-to-failure data for bearing prognostics.

An NSK 6205 steel-sealed ball bearing turned at 1770 to 1780 RPM with 300 kg (2.94 kN) of axial and 600 kg (5.88 kN) of vertical load, in cycles of 5 minutes of operation and 15 minutes with rest. The test ran for 128 hours and ended when the bearing temperature passed 85 °C and the vibration passed 9 m/s^2.

For size reasons the data was published at hourly intervals as 129 CSV files. Each file has four columns: x- and y-axis vibration from a PCB 352C34 sensor, bearing temperature and ambient temperature from K-type thermocouples, all sampled at 25.6 kHz for 78.125 seconds.
