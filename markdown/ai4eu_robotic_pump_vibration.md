## Description

This dataset comes from the robotic pump demonstrator of the AI4EU Robotics Pilot. The demonstrator is a hydraulic pump designed to sit on an industrial robot, for instance to feed liquid paint to a spray-painting process, and it carries one accelerometer for vibration monitoring. The record was published on Zenodo in 2021 by Morten Mossige of ABB Robotics, Ragesh Ramachandran of Fraunhofer IPA and Helge Spieker of Simula Research Laboratory.

The pump speed, set in rotations per minute, changes the paint throughput and the pressure in and out of the pump. The data holds 380 million measurements from several sensors of the pump system, taken at 1-second intervals over two months in 2020. It is split by recording date into 33 gzip-compressed CSV files and comes with a PDF documentation file. The dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.
