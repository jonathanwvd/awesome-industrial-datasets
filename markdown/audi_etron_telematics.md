# Electric vehicle telematics data from Audi e-tron SUVs in the United States

Over 10 million over-the-air records of state of charge, odometer and home or away status from 3100 Audi e-tron electric SUVs in the United States.

- **Domain:** Transportation and mobility
- **Asset / Process:** Vehicles and fleets
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Other
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Size:** Over 10 million measurements from 3100 vehicles
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY-ND and NC-ND

## Description

This dataset holds telematics records sent over the air by Audi e-tron electric SUVs in the United States. It accompanies the paper "Graph-based two-level clustering for electric vehicle usage patterns", published in the journal Energy and AI.

Each measurement has a timestamp, an anonymized vehicle identification number, a flag that tells whether the vehicle is at home or away, the odometer reading and the battery state of charge as a percentage of full capacity. Records arrive at irregular intervals, generally when the vehicle status changes, for example between driving and charging, so the number per vehicle varies. In total the data covers 3100 vehicles and over 10 million measurements in one Parquet file.

Version 2 was published on Mendeley Data in August 2025 under the Creative Commons Attribution NonCommercial NoDerivatives 4.0 license; it changes only the author attribution, not the data.
