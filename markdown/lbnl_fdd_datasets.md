# LBNL Fault Detection and Diagnostics Datasets

Time-series HVAC operating data for seven system types, each with faults at several severity levels and a fault-free case, for testing FDD algorithms.

- **Domain:** Buildings and appliances
- **Asset / Process:** HVAC and building systems
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Fault diagnosis
- **Annotation:** Class label
- **Source Type:** Mixed
- **Access:** Official portal
- **Size:** seven HVAC system types
- **Year:** 2022
- **Publisher:** Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

## Description

The LBNL Fault Detection and Diagnostics Datasets are a collection published by Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory through the Open Energy Data Initiative (OEDI) in 2022. They were produced by LBNL together with PNNL, NREL, ORNL and Drexel University to evaluate how accurately fault detection and diagnostics (FDD) algorithms and tools perform.

The operating data combine simulation runs, laboratory experiments and field measurements in real buildings. They cover seven kinds of HVAC equipment: rooftop units, single-duct and dual-duct air handling units, variable air volume boxes, fan coil units, chiller plants and boiler plants. For each system, CSV files hold time series for faults at different severity levels plus one fault-free case.

Every dataset also includes a PDF that documents its content and scope, and a TTL file that describes the data with the Brick schema. The work came from a DOE project on FDD test datasets and prioritization methods.
