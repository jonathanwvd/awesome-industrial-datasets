# SACAC

Industrial control loop data sets from SACAC, grouped by the cause of poor loop performance, with CSV and text files from chemical, paper, mineral and oil and gas plants.

- **Domain:** Cross-industrial
- **Asset / Process:** Valves and actuators
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Fault diagnosis; Process monitoring
- **Annotation:** Class label
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Publisher:** South African Council of Automation and Control (SACAC)

## Description

The SACAC data repository is an initiative of SACAC, the South African Council of Automation and Control. Its data comes from industrial control practitioners and from academic researchers who work closely with industry, and the repository is meant to serve as a test environment. A published paper describes it.

The data sets are arranged by the cause of poor control loop performance, using the categories of Bauer et al. (2016) ordered by how often they occur in industrial processes. Listed groups include plantwide, quantisation, saturation, sensor and other causes, plus loops whose cause is not identified.

File names give the cause, the industry, such as chemicals, paper, minerals and oil and gas, and an author name and year, for example Thornhill, Horch or Bauer. Most entries are a CSV file with a text file; some use MAT, PDF or XLS files. Industrial valve stiction data used in a comparative study by Bacci di Capaci et al. (2016) is also available, and the repository aims to encourage similar studies.
