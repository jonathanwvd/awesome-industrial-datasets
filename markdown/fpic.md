# FICS PCB Image Collection (FPIC)

261 high-resolution images of 93 printed circuit boards, front and back, with more than 71,000 annotated components and text instances for optical inspection.

- **Domain:** Electronics and semiconductors
- **Asset / Process:** PCBs and electronic assemblies
- **Modality:** Image
- **Task:** Other
- **Access:** Registration required
- **Size:** 261 images of 93 PCBs
- **Year:** 2023

## Description

The FICS PCB Image Collection (FPIC) is an image dataset for automated optical inspection of printed circuit boards and for hardware assurance of outsourced boards. It was introduced by Nathan Jessurun and co-authors in a paper accepted in February 2023, and it is distributed through the physicaldb data portal.

The collection has 261 high-resolution images of 93 boards, front and back, taken from hard drive controllers, servers, audio amplifiers and other equipment. More than 71,000 instances are annotated, covering mounted components and printed text; the download includes separate archives for component, text and other annotations plus colour-checker and metadata files.

The set serves training and evaluation of machine-learning inspection and hardware security methods. Download links are shown after registration on the portal. The publisher's keywords list automated optical inspection, semantic segmentation and hardware assurance.
