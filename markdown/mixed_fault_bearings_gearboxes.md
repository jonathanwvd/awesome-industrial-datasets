# Mixed fault dataset of bearings and gearboxes under variable operating conditions

Vibration signals of bearings and gearboxes under varying operating conditions, covering single bearing faults, compound ring faults, gear defects and mixed faults.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Bearings; Gears and gearboxes
- **Modality:** Vibration
- **Task:** Fault diagnosis
- **Access:** Mendeley Data
- **Year:** 2025
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Peoples Liberation Army Engineering University

## Description

This Mendeley Data record, contributed by Yinchuan Hou of the Peoples Liberation Army Engineering University, gathers vibration signals for several fault types in bearings and gearboxes running under changing operating conditions. Version 1 was published in July 2025 and is filed under intelligent fault diagnosis.

The bearing part holds single faults such as those of the inner ring, outer ring, rolling elements and cage, together with combined faults of the inner and outer rings. The gearbox part includes typical defects such as tooth surface wear, broken teeth and eccentricity. An additional set combines faults of bearings and gearboxes.

The contributor presents the collection as a reference for verifying intelligent diagnostic algorithms. It is released under CC BY 4.0.
