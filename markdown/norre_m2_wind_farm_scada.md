# SCADA data from Norre_m2 wind farm

Power, nacelle wind speed and yaw statistics from 42 turbines of the Norrekaer Enge wind farm, 1991 to 1993, with wind data from two masts.

- **Domain:** Energy and power
- **Asset / Process:** Wind turbines
- **Modality:** Time series
- **Task:** Other
- **Source Type:** Real production
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 42 turbines and 2 masts, 155.88 MB
- **Year:** 2022
- **License:** CC BY
- **Publisher:** Technical University of Denmark (DTU)

## Description

This dataset holds SCADA data and mast measurements from the 42 turbines of the Norrekaer Enge wind farm, an onshore coastal site. Kurt Schaldemose Hansen, Nikola Vasiljevic and Steen Arne Sørensen posted it on the DTU data repository in 2022.

The statistics cover more than one year, from December 1991 to June 1993, and include power, nacelle wind speed and yaw position for each turbine, plus wind speed and direction at hub height from two masts. The files are in NetCDF, 155.88 MB in total.

The record files the data under resource assessment, wind farm wakes and wind power plant performance, from a field experiment funded by CEC and EFP. It is released under CC BY 4.0.
