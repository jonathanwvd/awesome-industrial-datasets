## Description

This photovoltaic performance dataset supports the development of remote failure detection and diagnosis (FDD) algorithms that work on SCADA data. It comes from continuous monitoring, at TECNALIA's facilities, of seven PV modules whose failure modes are well characterized, using the LPVO-MS1X16 monitoring system developed by the University of Ljubljana. It was published on Zenodo in 2026, and a project deliverable shared through the SERENDIPV and CACTUS projects describes it.

Four of the modules are defective devices from dismantled Spanish PV plants, where visual and electroluminescence inspection found corrosion of the electrical circuit, broken interconnect ribbons, cracked solar cells and yellowed encapsulant. In two new modules, failures were created artificially by increasing the series resistance in one and decreasing the shunt resistance in the other. The seventh module is a new reference.

Individual maximum power point (MPP) trackers log the MPP voltage and current of each module every minute, and an IV tracer records each module's IV curve every five minutes. Module and air temperature, plane-of-array and global horizontal irradiance are measured synchronously. The data is released under CC BY 4.0.
