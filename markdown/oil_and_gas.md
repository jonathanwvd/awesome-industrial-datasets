# Oil and Gas Data, 1932-2014

Global dataset of oil and natural gas production, prices, exports and net exports by country, with oil series covering 1932 to 2014.

- **Domain:** Oil and gas
- **Modality:** Tabular; Time series
- **Task:** Forecasting
- **Access:** Zenodo, Dataverse or Figshare
- **Size:** 15,521 observations of 41 variables
- **Year:** 2015
- **License:** CC0 and public domain

## Description

Oil and Gas Data, 1932-2014 covers worldwide production, prices, exports and net exports of oil and natural gas. It was compiled by Michael Ross of the University of California, Los Angeles, and Paasha Mahdavi of Georgetown University. It was deposited on Harvard Dataverse in September 2015, and the current record is version 2.

Oil production and prices run from 1932 to 2014, with incomplete 2014 values; gas production and prices cover 1955 to 2014, and export and net export figures cover 1986 to 2013. Country codes were changed from earlier versions to follow the Correlates of War (COW) and Quality of Government (QOG) standards.

The main table has 41 variables and 15,521 observations. It is distributed as a tab-delimited file, originally in CSV format, together with a PDF codebook, and is released under CC0 1.0.
