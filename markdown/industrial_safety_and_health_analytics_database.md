## Description

The Industrial Safety and Health Analytics Database holds labor accident records from real-world manufacturing plants of a company that the contributors place among the biggest industries in Brazil and in the world. The stated reason for sharing it is the need for companies to understand why employees are still injured, and sometimes killed, in plants.

Each row is one accident from one of 12 plants in three countries. Columns give the date, the anonymized country and city, the industry sector, the accident level from I (not severe) to VI (very severe), the potential accident level, the gender of the person, whether they were an employee or a third party, the critical risk involved and a free-text account of how the accident happened.

Two CSV versions are provided, one of them with the accident descriptions, together with an example storyline image. The data is released under CC0 1.0.
