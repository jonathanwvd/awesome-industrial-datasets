# Co-Registered In-Situ and Ex-Situ Dataset of Electrical, Acoustic, and CT Characteristics from WAAM

Spatiotemporally registered welding, acoustic and X-ray CT data from the wire arc additive manufacturing deposition of one rectangular block, released by Oak Ridge National Laboratory.

- **Domain:** Manufacturing
- **Asset / Process:** Additive manufacturing; Welding
- **Modality:** Multimodal; Time series; X-ray and CT
- **Task:** Quality prediction
- **Access:** Official portal
- **Year:** 2024
- **Publisher:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory

## Description

Oak Ridge National Laboratory released this dataset in October 2024, with Vladimir Orlyanchik, Jeffrey Kimmell, Zack Snow, Amir Ziabari and Vincent Paquit as authors. It records the wire arc additive manufacturing (WAAM) deposition of a rectangular block, with every data stream registered to the others in space and time.

For that single build, the data covers a full account of the deposition process and its parameters, welding characteristics measured in situ, acoustic recordings and the results of X-ray computed tomography analysis. Its subject keywords also name weld current and an acoustic sensor.

ORNL sees integrated data analysis as an opportunity to improve precision, reduce waste and enhance quality in WAAM, and calls registration of in-situ, ex-situ and process data critical for AI/ML and optimisation models. Files are downloaded through Globus. UT-Battelle, which operates ORNL for the US Department of Energy, asserts no rights against users of the dataset and asks for academic attribution.
